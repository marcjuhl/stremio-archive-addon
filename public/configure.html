Stremio v3 Addon

Internet Archive Streaming

Stream movies, historical broadcasts, and series directly to Stremio with user-authenticated Archive.org sessions.

🔐 Authentication Method
How to get your S3 Keys: Log in to Archive.org, then visit archive.org/account/s3.php and generate your S3 API keys.
How to get your Cookies: Open your browser Developer Tools (F12) while logged in to Archive.org, go to Application > Cookies > archive.org, and copy logged_in_user and logged_in_sig.
Guest Mode: You can install and browse public domain collections without Archive.org credentials. Providing authentication unlocks higher rate limits and unrestricted item downloads.
