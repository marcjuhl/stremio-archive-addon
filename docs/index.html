🔐 Internet Archive Credentials
How to get S3 Keys: Log in to Archive.org, navigate to archive.org/account/s3.php and generate your S3 API credentials.
How to get Cookies: Open your browser Developer Tools (F12) while logged in to Archive.org, inspect Application > Cookies > archive.org, and copy logged_in_user and logged_in_sig.
